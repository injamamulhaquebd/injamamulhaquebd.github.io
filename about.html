My Journey

An experienced IT Technician with more than 9 years of hands-on experience in IT support, desktop troubleshooting, hardware and software maintenance, help desk operations, and computer networking. I take genuine satisfaction in solving technical problems and making technology work smoothly for people. Whether I am troubleshooting a hardware issue, configuring a computer, resolving software problems, investigating network connectivity, or assisting an end user, my approach is always based on patience, discipline, attention to detail, and a strong commitment to finding the right solution. I believe effective IT support is not simply about fixing a problem � it is about understanding the issue, preventing it from happening again, and providing users with a dependable technology experience.
